Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

WeHold is a free website and app published by WeHold LLC, a Tennessee limited liability company. This policy explains what information WeHold handles and where it goes. The short version: no account, no tracking, no ads.

What we collect

WeHold LLC does not sell personal information, show ads or use analytics or advertising trackers. There are no user accounts and no cookies set by WeHold. The only information that reaches WeHold LLC is described under "Phone notifications" below, and only if you turn notifications on.

What stays on your device

Your ZIP code, district, stance answers and the details you type into a letter (such as your name, town, email or phone) are saved only in your browser's or the app's local storage on your device so the app remembers them next time. They are never sent to WeHold LLC. You can erase them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Topics you follow

If you use WeHold Plus smart alerts, the app counts how many times you open or answer something on each topic (for example, "Energy & environment") so it can ask whether you'd like alerts on that topic. These counts, the topics you follow and the alerts you've seen are kept only on your device. They record what you read about, never which side you're on. You can see and clear them on the My topics screen.

Phone notifications

In the WeHold app for iPhone and Android, you can choose to get notifications about topics you follow. If you allow notifications, Apple or Google gives the app an anonymous push token, and the app sends WeHold's alert service only that token, whether the device is an iPhone or Android phone, and the topic ids you follow. We use them only to deliver WeHold notifications, which are the same for everyone following a topic. We do not receive your name, email, location, ZIP code, officials or answers. Unfollowing every topic, turning notifications off or deleting the app removes the token from our service. Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, and the alert service runs on Cloudflare.

WeHold Plus subscriptions

WeHold Plus is sold through the App Store or Google Play, which handle payment; WeHold LLC never sees your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase information from Apple or Google, under its own privacy policy. We do not link it to your name or any other information.

Information sent to other services

A few features need to look something up with an outside service. When you use them, your browser contacts that service directly:

  • U.S. Census Bureau Geocoder: when your ZIP code crosses district lines, the street address you enter is sent to the Census Bureau to find your congressional district.
  • Google Civic Information API: when you look up your polling place, the address you enter is sent to Google to find voting locations and times.
  • Your officials' contact forms: letters are sent by you, through each office's own website, phone line or mail. WeHold does not send or keep a copy.

Those services have their own privacy policies. WeHold LLC does not receive the results.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like most web hosts, these providers may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and the time of a visit, for security and operations. WeHold LLC does not use those logs to identify visitors.

Children

WeHold is meant for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
