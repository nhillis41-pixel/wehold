How this works

  • Your answers stay on your device. District and stance answers are saved only in this browser. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Matching compares your answers with the votes that directly test them: a bill question you answered, or a broad question a bill clearly tests. A bill is linked to a question by what it does, never by who sponsored it. Votes marked with a caution bundled several issues together, so a match there is less certain.
  • Suggested answers on bills come only from your own answers to the broad questions. They are never counted until you confirm them, and WeHold never labels anyone's politics.
  • Missed votes count only the votes tracked here. Congress keeps no formal attendance record, so a recorded "Not voting" is the standard measure. Members are not required to give a reason.
  • Procedural votes decide whether a bill moves forward, not whether it becomes law. In the Senate, most bills need 60 votes to clear that step.
  • Summaries are plain-language descriptions written from official records and news coverage. Use the links to read the official record and full bill text. Tennessee's new congressional map takes effect in January 2027; districts shown here are the current ones.
